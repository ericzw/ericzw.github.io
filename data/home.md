Zhongwei Qiu is an Algorithm Expert (**AliStar Top Talent Program**) at [Alibaba DAMO Academy](https://damo.alibaba.com/) and a Postdoctoral Researcher at [Zhejiang University](https://www.zju.edu.cn/), working with [Prof. Fei Wu](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=XJLn4MYAAAAJ&hl=en). He received his Ph.D. degree from the [University of Science and Technology Beijing](https://www.ustb.edu.cn/), jointly supervised by Prof. Dongmei Fu and [Dr. Jianlong Fu](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/people/jianf/) ([Microsoft Research Asia](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-asia/)), and his B.S. degree in 2018. He was a visiting researcher at the [University of Sydney](https://www.sydney.edu.au/) (2022–2023), supervised by [Dr. Chang Xu](http://changxu.xyz/). 

His research focuses on **Multimodal Learning**, **Large Vision-Language Models** and **Multimodal World Models**, including post-training for long-horizon reasoning via reinforcement learning, building autonomous agent frameworks, and constructing robust, observable evaluation systems and data synthesis pipelines. His earlier work spans 2D/3D human perception, video understanding, diffusion models, and low-level vision. He is passionate about developing simple yet effective models and methods that can be deployed in real-world products.
