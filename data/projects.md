AI Coach for Freestyle Skiing at 2022 Winter Olympics
**Role**: Primary algorithm developer for pose estimation and motion analysis.
AI coaching system "GuanJun" for freestyle skiing, developed with XiaoIce. It provides automated scoring, trajectory analysis, and long-term training tracking, helping athletes win 2 golds and 1 silver at the Beijing 2022 Winter Olympics.

Video Super-Resolution in Microsoft Edge
**Role**: Primary algorithm developer for the super-resolution model.
Our frequency-transformer-based video super-resolution (FTVSR) technology has been shipped to Microsoft Edge, using ML to remove compression artifacts and upscale low-resolution videos in real-time for millions of users.
