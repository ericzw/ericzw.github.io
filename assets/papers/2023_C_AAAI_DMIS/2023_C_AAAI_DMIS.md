# DMIS: Dynamic Mesh-based Importance Sampling for Training Physics-Informed Neural Networks

**Venue:** AAAI 2023 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Zijiang Yang, Zhongwei Qiu, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

Introduces dynamic mesh-based importance sampling to efficiently allocate training resources for physics-informed neural networks, accelerating convergence on complex PDEs.
