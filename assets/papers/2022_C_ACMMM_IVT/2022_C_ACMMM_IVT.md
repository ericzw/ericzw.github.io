# IVT: An End-to-End Instance-guided Video Transformer for 3D Pose Estimation

**Venue:** ACM MM 2022 (Oral) | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Zhongwei Qiu, Qiansheng Yang, Jian Wang, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

An instance-guided video transformer that performs end-to-end 3D human pose estimation by jointly modeling instance-level and temporal dependencies.
