# Learning Degradation-robust Spatiotemporal Frequency-Transformer for Video Super-Resolution

**Venue:** TPAMI | **Type:** Journal

**Authors:** Zhongwei Qiu, Huan Yang, Jianlong Fu, Daochang Liu, Chang Xu, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

A degradation-robust spatiotemporal frequency-transformer that exploits frequency-domain features for high-quality video super-resolution under various degradation conditions.
