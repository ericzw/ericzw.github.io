# HAP: Structure-Aware Masked Image Modeling for Human-Centric Perception

**Venue:** NeurIPS 2023 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Junkun Yuan, Xinyu Zhang, Hao Zhou, Jian Wang, Zhongwei Qiu, et al..

## Summary

Proposes structure-aware masked image modeling that explicitly encodes human body structure priors, achieving strong pre-training for human-centric perception tasks.
