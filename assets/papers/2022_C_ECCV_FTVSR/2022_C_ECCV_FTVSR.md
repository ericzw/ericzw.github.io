# Learning spatiotemporal frequency-transformer for compressed video super-resolution

**Venue:** ECCV 2022 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Zhongwei Qiu, Huan Yang, Jianlong Fu, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

Proposes a spatiotemporal frequency-transformer that operates in the frequency domain to restore high-quality frames from compressed video, shipped to Microsoft Edge Browser.
