# Multi-scale contrastive adaptor learning for segmenting anything in underperformed scenes

**Venue:** Neurocomputing | **Type:** Journal

**Authors:** Ke Zhou, Zhongwei Qiu, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

Introduces multi-scale contrastive adaptors to extend the Segment Anything Model (SAM) to challenging underperformed scenes with improved generalization.
