# Dynamic Graph Reasoning for Multi-person 3D Pose Estimation

**Venue:** ACM MM 2022 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Zhongwei Qiu, Qiansheng Yang, Jian Wang, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

Applies dynamic graph reasoning to model inter-person interactions for improved multi-person 3D pose estimation in crowded scenes.
