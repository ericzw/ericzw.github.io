# MM-NeRF: Multimodal-Guided 3D Multi-Style Transfer of Neural Radiance Field

**Venue:** TVCG | **Type:** Journal

**Authors:** Zijiang Yang, Zhongwei Qiu, Chang Xu, Dongmei Fu.

## Summary

Enables multimodal-guided 3D style transfer on Neural Radiance Fields, allowing text and image-driven artistic stylization of 3D scenes.
