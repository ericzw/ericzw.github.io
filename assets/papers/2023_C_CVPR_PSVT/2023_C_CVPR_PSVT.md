# PSVT: End-to-End Multi-person 3D Pose and Shape Estimation with Progressive Video Transformers

**Venue:** CVPR 2023 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Zhongwei Qiu, Yang Qiansheng, Jian Wang, Haocheng Feng, Junyu Han, Errui Ding, Chang Xu, Dongmei Fu, Jingdong Wang.

## Summary

An end-to-end progressive video transformer framework for simultaneous multi-person 3D pose and shape estimation from monocular video.
