# From Histopathology Images to Cell Clouds: Learning Slide Representations with Hierarchical Cell Transformer

**Venue:** Preprint | **Type:** Preprint

**Authors:** Zijiang Yang*, Zhongwei Qiu*, Tiancheng Lin, Hanqing Chao, et al..

## Summary

Proposes a hierarchical cell transformer that converts histopathology images into cell cloud representations, learning multi-scale slide-level features for pathological analysis.
