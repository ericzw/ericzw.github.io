# Pedestrian-Centric 3D Pre-collision Pose and Shape Estimation from Dashcam Perspective

**Venue:** NeurIPS 2024 | **Type:** Conference

**Authors:** Meijun Wang, Yu Meng, Zhongwei Qiu, Chao Zheng, Yan Xu, Xiaorui Peng, Jiao Gao.

## Summary

Estimates 3D pose and body shape of pedestrians in pre-collision scenarios from dashcam footage, enabling safety-critical applications in autonomous driving.
